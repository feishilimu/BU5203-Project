# Product: RegRadar (v2)
Agentic research assistant for a bank's Internal Audit team (sponsor: DBS). For a chosen topic
and jurisdictions, an AI discovery agent searches allowed regulator domains and recommends
documents; a human chooses which to read; each document is profiled (issue/effective dates,
status, scope, key requirements, internal areas affected); results appear as a key-dates
tracker, a cross-jurisdiction comparison and a grounded Q&A; a human approves the report.

Topics (topic packs): data protection & cross-border transfer (default), financial crime
(AML/CFT), scam & fraud liability (v1). Jurisdictions: DBS markets (SG, HK, IN, ID, CN) and
benchmarks (UK, AU) for scams.

Non-negotiables: official domains only (plus human-added); agent guardrails in code; dates
verified against documents; human gates at source selection and approval; full audit trail;
read-only tools; responsible scraping. No compliance judgements.
